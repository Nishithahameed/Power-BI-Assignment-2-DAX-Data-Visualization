# Power-BI-Assignment-2-DAX-Data-Visualization
 In this assignment,  used DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) to analyze and transform e-commerce sales data, generating key metrics and insights.
 Created compelling visualizations and performed detailed analysis to gain valuable insights into sales trends,
 profitability, and performance metrics within the e-commerce domain.
 Ensured that relationships are established between the necessary tables.

Calculated Columns:
 Created Calculated Column for 'Category Type',' Revenue per Order', Sales Category’

Calculated Measures:
Calculated 'Order Count' ,'Average Profit in Delhi' ,'Calculate Year-to-Date'.

Data Visualization:
Sales Target Achievement by Category: Compared actual sales with sales targets by
category using a clustered column chart.

Max Profit Margin by Sub-Category: Analyzed the maximum profit margin for each
sub-category of products using a donut chart.

Monthly Sales Trend: displayed the trend of monthly sales over time using a line chart.

Comparison of Profit and Quantity by Sub-Category: Compared the relationship between
profit and quantity sold for different sub-categories using a scatter chart.

Comparison of Total Sales Amount and Target: Created cards to display the total
sales amount alongside the sales target for quick comparison and analysis. Also, created a
multi-row card to display the minimum target for each segment.

Sales Performance Matrix: Created a matrix view to analyze how actual sales compare to
sales targets across different categories and months.

Geographic Sales Analysis: Visualized the total sales on a map by city to identify regional sales
patterns.

Sales Distribution by Sub-Category: created the sales distribution across different
sub-categories using a treemap.

Order Count Analysis by State: Created a funnel chart to visualize the distribution of order
counts across different states.

After creating the charts, formatted and
customized them to enhance visual appeal and comprehension.
